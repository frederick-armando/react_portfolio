# Masteos - Case Study Summary

## Context

Masteos wanted to democratize real estate investment for individual users. The published case study describes a B2C mobile and desktop redesign focused on making a complex investment journey more accessible.

## Product problem

The user journey was fragmented and intimidating, with complex financial information that needed to be simplified without hiding useful decision-making context.

## Role

Frederick Armando worked as Product Designer on UX/UI redesign, user research, journey mapping, wireframes, high-fidelity design, specifications and product iterations.

## Published outcomes

- 32% increase in finalized investments
- 20% drop in bounce rate on property pages
- +15 point NPS increase
- More than 120 user interviews referenced in the published case study
