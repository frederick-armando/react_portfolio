# Mobioos - Case Study Summary

## Context

Mobioos is a B2B SaaS and no-code case study. The published content describes work on Forge and Fusion, platforms intended to make enterprise application customization more accessible.

## Product problem

The product challenge was to translate complex technical architecture, including UML and AI generation, into an experience usable through chatbot interaction and drag-and-drop customization.

## Role

Frederick Armando worked as UX Designer while also working on Kirrk. The published content describes interface design, prototyping, documentation, product collaboration and UX work for non-technical customization flows.

## Published focus areas

- No-code customization
- B2B SaaS interface design
- Chatbot-assisted configuration
- Drag-and-drop customization
- Translation of technical architecture into accessible UX
