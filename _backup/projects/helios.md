# Helios - Case Study Summary

## Context

Helios is an internal Masteos back-office case study. The published content describes a period where internal teams were slowed by inefficient tools and fragmented workflows while Masteos was scaling.

## Product problem

Internal teams needed a clearer operational tool for handling investment workflows, property information and day-to-day collaboration.

## Role

Frederick Armando worked as Product Designer on UX/UI design, interviews with internal users, co-design workshops, wireframes, prototypes, documentation and delivery with product and engineering teams.

## Published outcomes

- 40% reduction in investment processing time
- Internal NPS increased from 6.5 to 8.7
- 4 co-design workshops referenced in the published case study
