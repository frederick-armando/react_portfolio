# Frederick Armando — Methods

## Product design approach

Frederick Armando describes his product design approach as a way to turn ambiguous topics into clear, discussable and measurable product decisions.

## Frame

Turn an ambiguous topic into an actionable problem by clarifying goals, target users, constraints and success criteria before producing solutions.

## Align

Bring business goals, user needs and technical feasibility together. Workshops, maps and prototypes make decisions visible and discussable.

## Design

Move quickly from hypothesis to prototype. Structure flows, interactions and interfaces with a strong focus on clarity, accessibility and scalability.

## Deliver

Secure the transition from design to implementation by documenting behaviors, states, usage rules and guidelines for product and engineering teams.

## Measure

Evaluate the real impact of solutions through user feedback, usage signals and available KPIs to prioritize the next iterations.
