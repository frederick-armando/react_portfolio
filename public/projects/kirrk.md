# Kirrk - Case Study Summary

## Context

Kirrk is a mobility and car-sharing case study. The published content describes a multi-platform ecosystem covering B2C and B2B applications plus a B2B dashboard.

## Product problem

The product challenge was to simplify fleet use, vehicle booking, keyless access and digital vehicle workflows while supporting operational fleet management.

## Role

Frederick Armando worked as UX/UI Designer over 2.5 years. The published content describes design work across mobile apps, SaaS flows, dashboards, prototyping, handoff and UX analysis.

## Published outcomes

- Fleet utilization rate above 80%
- Keyless Bluetooth access
- Digital condition reports
- Cross-platform B2B/B2C ecosystem
