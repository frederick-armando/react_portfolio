# Michelin MyXpert - Case Study Summary

## Context

Michelin B2B partners and sales teams were working across a fragmented ecosystem of applications. The published case study describes a pivot from a mobile web view concept toward a native application.

## Product problem

The previous ecosystem created diluted communication, user frustration and friction for sales teams who needed reliable technical information during client interactions.

## Role

Frederick Armando worked as Lead Product Designer. The published content describes work on product framing, native mobile strategy, information architecture, design system work, wireframing, user testing, handoff and alignment with product, business and engineering teams.

## Published outcomes

- Centralized sales companion launched in the United States and China
- 3 redundant legacy applications decommissioned
- Rollout target of 20,000 active users across 12 countries by 2026
