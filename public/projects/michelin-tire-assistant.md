# Michelin Tire Assistant — Case Study Summary

## Context

Michelin's existing B2C chatbot was a passive support tool, triggered only by the user. The published challenge was to turn it into a proactive assistant able to intervene at the right moment in the journey.

## Product problem

The chatbot entry point limited adoption because it could be perceived as a report button or survey rather than a conversational assistant. The product answered requests but did not guide users enough toward the right next action.

## Role

Frederick Armando worked as Lead Product Designer. His published role included framing the problem with product and engineering teams, mapping conversational flows, designing contextual notifications, advocating for a more identifiable entry point and documenting behavior for handoff.

## Constraints

- Keep compatibility with the existing experience
- Avoid cannibalizing organic SEO traffic
- Work in an international context
- Cover journey structure, interactions and specifications without a dedicated UI designer

## Key decisions

- Make the assistant easier to identify
- Introduce contextual moments of intervention
- Rework scenarios to avoid dead ends
- Guide users without interrupting the existing journey

## Trade-offs

The proactive layer had to increase engagement without becoming intrusive. Notifications needed to stay contextual, readable and limited to moments where they could support a decision, while preserving the existing experience and organic SEO.

## Solution

The redesign turned the chatbot into a more proactive assistant with contextual suggestions, better-timed welcome messages, fallback paths and recommendations adapted to detected needs. The design was built to guide without interrupting, and to help users move from a question to an action.

## Published impact

- +18% open rate
- 21.3% purchase intent

## Learnings

The case study states that strong conversational UX depends on more than answer quality. Timing, role clarity and the connection between user need, product decision and business impact are central.
